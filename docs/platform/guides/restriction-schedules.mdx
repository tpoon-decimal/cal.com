# Restriction Schedules

A Restriction Schedule is an advanced availability feature that allows you to add an additional layer of constraints on when bookings can be made for a specific event type. It operates separately from your regular availability schedule.

## How Restriction Schedules Work

When a Restriction Schedule is configured on an event type, the available booking slots are determined by the **intersection** of:

1. Your regular availability schedule (e.g., 9am–5pm)
2. The Restriction Schedule's allowed times

This means a time slot must fall within **both** schedules to be bookable. If either schedule blocks a time, that slot will not be available.

### Example

If your regular availability is **9am–5pm** and your Restriction Schedule allows bookings only between **10am–3pm**, then only slots between **10am–3pm** will be available to bookers.

## Timezone Considerations

Restriction Schedules have specific timezone behavior that can affect which slots are available:

- **Schedule's timezone**: By default, the restriction schedule is evaluated in the schedule's own timezone.
- **Booker's timezone**: When configured to use the booker's timezone, the restrictions are applied based on the booker's local time.

This timezone setting can cause slots that appear available in one timezone to be blocked in another. For example, if your restriction schedule is set to 10am–4pm in UTC and a booker is in a timezone that's UTC+4, the available hours will shift accordingly.

## Configuring Restriction Schedules

To view or modify a Restriction Schedule:

1. Go to your **Event Type** settings.
2. Look for the **Restriction Schedule** option in the availability section.
3. Review or adjust the schedule's hours and timezone settings.
4. Save your changes.

To remove restrictions, unlink the restriction schedule from your event type.

<Note>
  The Restriction Schedule feature may need to be enabled for your account. If you don't see this option, contact support for assistance.
</Note>

## "Not allowed by restriction schedule" Error

If you or your bookers encounter a **"Not allowed by restriction schedule"** error, it means the requested booking time falls outside the allowed hours defined by the Restriction Schedule — even if it appears to be within your regular availability.

To resolve this:

1. Check if a Restriction Schedule is configured on the event type.
2. Review the Restriction Schedule's hours and compare them to your regular availability.
3. Verify the timezone settings match your intended availability.
4. Either adjust the Restriction Schedule hours or remove it if it is not needed.
