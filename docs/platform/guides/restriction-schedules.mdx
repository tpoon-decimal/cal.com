title: Restriction Schedules
description: Learn how Restriction Schedules apply additional time restrictions on top of availability for team event types
---

Restriction Schedules are an optional feature for team event types that applies an additional layer of booking restrictions on top of your hosts' regular availability. When enabled, a Restriction Schedule filters available time slots so that only times falling within **both** the host's availability **and** the restriction schedule are bookable.

## How Restriction Schedules Work

A Restriction Schedule acts as a policy filter that narrows the bookable window. The system calculates the intersection of the host's availability and the restriction schedule to determine which slots are offered to bookers.

For example:
- Host availability: **9:00 AM – 5:00 PM** (Europe/London)
- Restriction Schedule: **10:00 AM – 6:00 PM** (Asia/Dubai)

Only time slots that fall within both windows are bookable. Slots outside the restriction schedule window are excluded, even if the host is available.

If a booking is attempted outside the restriction schedule hours, it will be rejected with the error **"Not allowed by restriction schedule."**

## Availability

Restriction Schedules are available for **team event types** only. Individual and managed event types do not support restriction schedules.

## Configuring a Restriction Schedule

To configure a Restriction Schedule for a team event type:

1. Navigate to your **Event Type** settings.
2. Open the **Availability** tab.
3. Toggle on **"Restrict schedule for hosts"** to enable the restriction schedule.
4. Select a schedule from the dropdown to use as the restriction schedule. The dropdown lists all available schedules except the one already selected as the main schedule.
5. Save your changes.

When the toggle is turned off, the restriction schedule is removed and all bookable slots are determined solely by the host's availability.

## Use Booker Timezone

The Restriction Schedule section includes a **"Use Booker Timezone"** checkbox that controls how the restriction hours are evaluated:

- **Unchecked (default)**: The restriction schedule hours are interpreted in the timezone configured on the restriction schedule itself. For example, if the restriction schedule is set to 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM in Asia/Dubai, those hours are always evaluated in Asia/Dubai time regardless of where the booker is located.

- **Checked**: The restriction schedule hours are interpreted in the booker's local timezone. For example, if the restriction schedule is set to 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM and the booker is in Asia/Kolkata, the restriction is applied as 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM Kolkata time.

This setting affects which slots are available and can lead to different results depending on the timezone difference between the restriction schedule and the booker.

## Common Use Cases

Restriction Schedules are useful when you want to:

- Limit bookings to specific business hours across all hosts on a team event, regardless of individual host availability.
- Enforce organizational policies that restrict booking windows beyond what individual availability allows.
- Apply different booking windows for different team event types while hosts maintain the same underlying availability schedules.
